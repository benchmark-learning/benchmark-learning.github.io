Your privacy

BenchMark keeps your work on your own device. It doesn't know who you are, and nothing you type or do is sent to your teacher, the school or anyone else.

The short version

No sign-upNo account, no email, no password. BenchMark doesn't know who you are.
Your progress stays on this deviceIt's saved in this browser only. Nobody else can see it, including your teacher, unless you choose to share your progress link.
No AIAnswers are marked on your device by fixed rules, written from the SQA marking instructions and checked by your teacher. No AI reads your work.
Nothing is sent anywhereYour answers and marks never leave this device. No tracking, no cookies, no adverts. The page itself tells your browser to refuse any connection apart from BenchMark's own files and YouTube videos.
Adding it to your home screenIt just opens BenchMark faster and lets it work offline. Nothing extra is saved or shared.

What is kept, and where

WhatWhereWho can see it
Your answers, marks, topic progress, revision plan, Design lab and game progressThis browser on this deviceOnly you
A random nickname and the class you pickedThis browser on this deviceOnly you
Your progress linkWherever you choose to send it (for example your own Teams chat)You, and anyone you send it to

What leaves your device

Nothing you type or do. Two things touch the internet, as with any website:

WhenWhat happens
Pages load from the websiteThe company that hosts BenchMark (GitHub) sees your device's internet address, as every website host does. BenchMark itself never sees or keeps it.
You press play on a CAD videoThe video comes from YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode), so YouTube's own privacy rules apply to it.

Your choices

Delete your progressIn BenchMark, tap Your privacy at the foot of the page, then Clear my progress on this device. Clearing your browser data does the same.
Move your progressUse Switch device at the top right. Your progress travels in a link that you send to yourself; it holds no name.
Keep personal details outPlease don't type your name or other personal details into answers.
